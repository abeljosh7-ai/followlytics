📥 How to Download Your Instagram Data

1

Open your Instagram app. Go to your profile and tap the three-line menu (☰).

2

Open Accounts Center.

3

Select Your information and permissions.

4

Tap Download your information, then choose Download or transfer information.

5

Select your Instagram account. Choose Some of your information. Select Followers and following.

6

Choose Download to device. Select JSON format and choose All time if available. Request your download.

7

When Instagram notifies you that your download is ready, download the ZIP file to your computer.

⚠️ Important: Choose JSON format, not HTML. Upload the original ZIP file without extracting it.
